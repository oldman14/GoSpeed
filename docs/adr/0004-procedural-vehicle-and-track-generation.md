# Generate 3D Vehicles and Circuit Geometry Procedurally

We will build the 3D kart meshes, city environment, and race track procedurally using Three.js geometry primitives, custom materials, and shader effects rather than relying on external GLTF/GLB models. This makes the game 100% self-contained, guarantees zero asset loading time, and gives us programmatic control over articulated wheel steering, chassis body roll, underglow lighting, and particle emission anchors.
